Buck–boost power stage study

Smaller, lower-power 3.3 V rail from a single Li-ion cell, and a plan to prove it.

Where
Emergence of Communication Lab
Areas
Power electronics

Tools

  • TPS63060
  • TPS63802
  • Power analyzer
  • Electronic load
  • Thermal camera

Documents

  • Characterization plan (PDF)

The board needs a regulated 3.3 V rail from a single-cell Li-ion battery (2.9 to 4.2 V). I evaluated whether a newer regulator could shrink the power stage without giving anything up.

Decision rule: choose the smallest power stage that meets every limit at minimum input voltage and maximum load, not just the smallest inductor.

Current versus proposed

Input range
TPS63060: 2.5–12 V. TPS63802: 1.3–5.5 V, which covers the whole cell range.
Footprint
9 mm² down to 6 mm².
Quiescent current
30 µA down to 11 µA.
Reference inductor
1 µH down to 0.47 µH.

How I'd validate it

  • Emulate the battery with a current-limited supply at 2.9, 3.3, 3.7 and 4.2 V.
  • Sweep the load from idle through nominal to maximum with an electronic load.
  • Capture load-step undershoot, overshoot and settling on the scope.
  • Measure efficiency, ripple and switching spikes.
  • Check inductor peak current against Isat, and IC and inductor temperatures.
