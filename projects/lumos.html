LUMOS

A 48-LED display with an ESP32, two LED drivers and a printed enclosure.

Where
Personal build
Areas
Embedded, PCB design, Mechanical

Tools

  • ESP32
  • TLC5947
  • 3D printing
  • DB25 harness

A 48-LED display driven by an ESP32 through two TLC5947 constant-current drivers, with brightness and button controls. It runs from an AA pack through a 5 V buck.

Total BOM under $50.

Build

  • Two TLC5947 drivers with IREF resistors setting LED current.
  • Six AA cells (9 V nominal) into a 5 V, 2 A buck, with bulk and decoupling capacitors.
  • A pot for brightness, three pushbuttons and a power switch, all on terminal blocks.
  • A DB25 cable harness between the board and the display.

Enclosure fit testing

  1. v0. LED and M3 screw fit only. The M3 hole was too big and the LED hole too small.
  2. v1. Added the pot, switch and button cutouts. The pushbutton fit was perfect; the others needed adjusting.
  3. v2. Updated all the fits, then printed the display, holder and cover.
