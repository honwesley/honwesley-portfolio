Supermileage driver UI board

The driver's control panel and a node on the car's CAN bus.

Where
Bruin Supermileage SAE
Role
KiCad UI PCB team lead

A central driver interface for lights, horn, wipers, ignition and kill switch. It also serves as a communication node on the vehicle's CAN network.

Design

  • An ESP32 handles GPIO inputs, CAN, and Bluetooth for logging and configuration.
  • Driver inputs have signal conditioning and debouncing.
  • MOSFET and relay outputs switch the 12 V loads.
  • Reverse-polarity and fuse protection, plus 12 V to 5 V and 3.3 V conversion.
  • An I2C LCD/OLED and indicator LEDs give the driver feedback.

Leading the team

Directed bring-up, component selection, power distribution and electrical interface reviews across the mechanical subteams.
