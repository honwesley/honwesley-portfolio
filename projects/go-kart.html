Verstappenmobiel electric go-kart

A 36 V, two-motor kart with Ackermann steering and an F1-style wheel.

Where
Team of six

A team-built electric kart. I worked on the electronics.

Electronics

  • An ESC powered by a 36 V battery drives both motors: speed, direction and braking.
  • A potentiometer at the end of the steering rod measures steering angle.
  • An OLED on the F1-inspired steering wheel shows steering angle and speed.
  • The microcontroller and wiring are soldered onto perfboard and sealed in a box behind the seat.

Chassis

Designed for 50/50 left-right weight, ideally 40/60 front-rear. The front wheels mount above the chassis to center the weight, and the seat position came from measuring our drivers.
