Distributed detection in Gaussian noise

How many noisy sensors does it take to get the error rate under 10⁻³?

When
June 2026
Where
ECE 131A extra credit project

Detecting a signal of amplitude 0.5 with many sensors in Gaussian noise. I compared full-precision sensors against cheaper one-bit sensors and optimized the decision threshold.

Results

Full-precision sensors
39 sensors reach an error probability of 8.97 × 10⁻⁴, under the 10⁻³ target. A Monte Carlo run of about 220,000 trials confirmed it.
Binary sensors
61 one-bit sensors needed for the same target, also checked by simulation.
Cost trade-off
Compared total cost at each target error rate to find where cheaper binary sensors win.
Threshold tuning
Optimizing a modified threshold (γ ≈ −0.81) lowered the error with a fixed sensor count.
